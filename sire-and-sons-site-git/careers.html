Line cook

Kitchen

What you'll do

  • Prep, cook, and plate menu items to our recipes
  • Keep your station clean, stocked, and ready
  • Follow food safety rules every shift
  • Work with the pit crew and front counter to keep orders moving

Who we're looking for

  • Kitchen experience helps, and we'll train the right person
  • Able to work fast and stay on your feet
  • Texas Food Handler card, or willing to get one
  • Reliable, on time, and good with a team

Cashier

Front counter

What you'll do

  • Greet guests, take orders, and handle payments
  • Answer questions about the menu
  • Keep the counter and dining area tidy
  • Tell the kitchen what's coming so orders go out right

Who we're looking for

  • Friendly, patient, and easy to talk to
  • Cash or point-of-sale experience helps
  • Calm and accurate when it gets busy
  • Reliable and on time

Dishwasher / busser

Kitchen and dining room

What you'll do

  • Wash, sanitize, and put away dishes, pans, and utensils
  • Clear and reset tables quickly
  • Help keep the kitchen clean and organized
  • Pitch in wherever the team needs a hand

Who we're looking for

  • No experience needed, a strong work ethic matters most
  • Able to stand, bend, and lift through a shift
  • Quick, careful, and dependable

Pit crew

Smokers and prep

What you'll do

  • Prep, season, and load meat for the smokers
  • Tend the fire and keep wood and supplies stocked
  • Slice, portion, and hold finished meat to our standards
  • Keep the pit area clean and follow food safety rules

Who we're looking for

  • BBQ or smoker experience helps, and passion for the craft matters most
  • Comfortable with early starts, heat, and heavy lifting
  • Texas Food Handler card, or willing to get one
  • Patient and detail-minded, ready to learn from the pitmaster
